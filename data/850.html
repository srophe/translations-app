1.1. A Writing of2 Mar Cyriac Bishop of Amrna About the Same3 Holy Mar James 4.

When then Mar James the bishop from the holy convent of Fsiltha had departed to our Lord a considerable time before, and the patriarch Mar Athanasius had arisen upon the see of Peter the great5, Phocas king of the Romans again expelled the orthodox (όρθόδοξοι) bishops from their sees and appointed heretics6, but by the judgments of God the Persian king Khosrau 269615son of Hormizd came and took all the country east of the Euphrates in war7; and by God's provision and queen Shirin's advice he8 expelled the bishops of Chalcedon from the cities east of the Euphrates, and appointed orthodox (όρθόδοξοι) bishops in their places, viz. in Amida Mar Samuel, and in Edessa Mar Isaiah, and in Rhesaina Mar Addai, and in Thella Mar Zakkhai; and so in every district and city9, while they agreed in everything with the blessed patriarch. At this same time therefore, when Mar Zakkhai bishop of Thella had heard and learned of the holiness of the blessed Mar James, he was zealous for the Lord and for that holy soul and exerted and bestirred himself, and sent four blessed men from his convent, and two of the clergymen of the city (and he supplied them with a swift animal and money) to go to the monastery of Casium in which he fell asleep, and steal him10 thence. And he laid down a law (νόμος) for them as to how they should act and dispatched them. Now those who went were sent in the year nine hundred and thirty-three of the Greeks 11. And at the same time Heraclius king of the Romans 12 came, and took all the country east of the Euphrates, and drove the Persians as far as 270616 Nisibis which is on the frontier13. And14 the members of the monastery went in the days of the devout Mar Stephen who was termed Shbiliya15; and, having gone and arrived at the monastery of Casium, they went into the monastery and prayed and came near16 and were blessed by the holy Mar James, and by17 the brothers of the house. And on being asked whither they were travelling they answered ‘We are envoys of the church of Syria to the pope. of Alexandria.’ Then they were received with much honour. When then18 they had remained there two days, in accordance with a plan and trick that had been concerted by them on the way, one of these monks19, the envoys, set up a wail being possessed by a demon20 and foaming and went out of his mind21. And his companions began to be distressed and weep; and in sorrow they went on to fasten their companion to the sarcophagus of the 271617holy Mar James, in order that he might learn how22 to steal that holy body, while his companions on his account slept23 with him close by the sarcophagus itself. But during the night they would bore, and in the day cover the borings that they had made. And, when the task had become easy for them, they took the keys (κλείδα) of the monastery to themselves, that their task might be easily accomplished24. Then they said to the members of the monastery of Casium, ‘We, sirs25, since we have a letter with us, cannot remain here with this sick man26; but we will leave one of us with him and a swift animal, in order that, when the Lord pleases and he recovers, he may quickly join us at Alexandria, in the holy convent of Marth Zoila 27, but pray for us’. And, having gone thence, they came as far as Palestine on their return; and the man who had feigned madness and his companion rose by night and took the body of the holy Mar James, and wrapped it in silk shrouds, and came on the swift animal to their companions in Palestine according to the agreement that they had made with one another.

But the blessed men the members of the monastery of Casium, when they rose in the morning and saw that the saint's body had been taken away, lifted up their voice in weeping and in sobs; and they went in all directions after them, and did not find them28; and they returned to their monastery mourning. But the blessed men departed from Palestine, themselves and the clergymen, and rapidly arrived at the city of Thella, carrying his holy body with them. And, when the bishop29 and the clergy and the citizens30 heard of it, they went to meet it with lights and incense and songs of praise, and carried it round the whole city, accompanying it with spiritual odes, and chants of the Holy Spirit. And then they31 wrapped it round and laid it in his monastery, the convent of Fsiltha, in the temple which the saint had built in his lifetime32, and it is there for the assistance and healing of all men33, and for the honour and praise of Christ his34 God; 273619to whom and to his Father with his35 Holy Spirit be praise and honour and worship and exaltation, now and always and36 for ever and ever. Amen!
